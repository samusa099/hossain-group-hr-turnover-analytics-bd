# Executive Mandate

## Primary mandate

Determine whether the current workforce situation represents an isolated turnover issue or a broader failure involving workforce planning, management control, policy governance, employee experience and organisational oversight.

## Questions from management

Your submission must address the questions that are relevant to your selected role.

1. Which management statements are supported by the current evidence?
2. Which statements are plausible but unverified?
3. Which matters cannot be assessed from the available fields?
4. Has workforce growth produced stability, or has replacement activity concealed continued movement?
5. Are material patterns concentrated by department, location, employment type, tenure, gender or another available segment?
6. Which exits appear structural, and which may require further retention investigation?
7. Are the current policy, approval and record-keeping controls sufficient to support consistent management decisions?
8. Which attendance, leave, promotion, performance, grievance or manager-level questions require additional evidence?
9. Which current Bangladesh labour-law or regulatory considerations may be relevant to the situation?
10. Can compliance be assessed from the available project evidence, or is additional documentation required?
11. Which actions are justified immediately?
12. Which actions should not be taken before further investigation?
13. What should management review after 90 days and after 180 days?
14. How should HR information governance be improved so that the same uncertainty does not recur?

## Required decision layers

### Layer 1 — Establish the facts

Validate the source data, calculations and reported workforce indicators.

### Layer 2 — Define the management problem

Frame the issue without assuming that recruitment, retention, policy, leadership or employee behaviour is the sole cause.

### Layer 3 — Test competing explanations

Classify management claims as:

- supported by current evidence;
- plausible but unconfirmed;
- not assessable from current evidence.

### Layer 4 — Recommend action

Separate recommendations into:

- immediate controls;
- targeted investigation;
- longer-term capability and governance improvement.

### Layer 5 — Establish accountability

For each recommendation, define:

- owner;
- timing;
- evidence required;
- success measure;
- review point;
- risk of inaction.

### Layer 6 — Communicate from the selected role

The final submission must reflect the authority, responsibilities and limitations of the declared role.

## Final objective

> Deliver a management-ready position that management can defend, act on and review over time.

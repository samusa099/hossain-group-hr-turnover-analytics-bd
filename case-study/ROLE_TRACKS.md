# Role Tracks

Select one primary role. A hybrid response is allowed, but one role must remain accountable for the final decision.

## Managing Director

**Decision authority:** Enterprise prioritisation and executive approval.

Your response should emphasise:

- the organisation-level problem definition;
- decisions that can be approved now;
- decisions that require further evidence;
- management accountability;
- policy and governance risk;
- 90-day and 180-day review expectations.

Required brief: [`variants/managing-director.md`](variants/managing-director.md)

## HR Business Partner

**Decision authority:** Department intervention, employee-relations response and management support.

Your response should emphasise:

- department and segment patterns;
- competing causes;
- evidence versus allegation;
- targeted intervention;
- manager and employee follow-up;
- an investigation and measurement plan.

Required brief: [`variants/hr-business-partner.md`](variants/hr-business-partner.md)

## People Analytics

**Decision authority:** Analytical method, evidence quality and reporting design.

Your response should emphasise:

- data validation;
- metric reconciliation;
- segmentation;
- dashboard or analytical reporting;
- limitations;
- reproducibility and decision usefulness.

Required brief: [`variants/people-analytics.md`](variants/people-analytics.md)

## Operations and Finance

**Decision authority:** Workforce continuity, hiring control and resource prioritisation.

Your response should emphasise:

- gross hiring versus net growth;
- replacement pressure;
- operational continuity;
- department prioritisation;
- transparent assumptions;
- scenario-based management choices.

Required brief: [`variants/operations-finance.md`](variants/operations-finance.md)

## Policy and Governance

**Decision authority:** Policy control, documentation, approval consistency and compliance review.

Your response should emphasise:

- policy clarity;
- evidence and record controls;
- attendance, leave, promotion and grievance governance;
- current official Bangladesh legal and regulatory research;
- compliance evidence gaps;
- auditability and accountability.

Required brief: [`variants/policy-governance.md`](variants/policy-governance.md)

## Hybrid track

A participant may combine two tracks, for example:

- Managing Director + Policy and Governance
- HR Business Partner + People Analytics
- Operations and Finance + People Analytics

The submission must identify:

```text
Primary role:
Secondary role:
Final decision owner:
```

A hybrid response must not avoid accountability by presenting separate conclusions from each role.

# Management Context

The statements below are management inputs, not established facts. Participants must test them against the available evidence and identify where further data is required.

## Managing Director

The organisation has approved recruitment repeatedly, yet several functions continue to report workforce pressure. Management needs one defensible position rather than separate departmental explanations.

Key concern:

> Is the organisation expanding successfully, or is workforce growth concealing instability?

## Chief Operating Officer

Operational managers report gaps in continuity, delayed handovers and inconsistent workforce availability. The COO believes attendance discipline and line-management control may be material factors.

Evidence limitation:

The current project does not contain attendance, shift, overtime, leave-approval or productivity records.

## Finance Head

Finance is concerned about the difference between gross recruitment and net workforce growth. The Finance Head wants to know whether new hiring is supporting expansion, replacing avoidable exits or compensating for structural contract movement.

Evidence limitation:

The current project does not contain recruitment cost, replacement cost, salary, payroll or productivity-value data.

## Group Head of HR

HR sees several possible explanations: external opportunity, career expectations, employment structure, supervisor experience, work-life balance and inconsistent policy application. HR is not prepared to treat informal comments as proven causes.

Key concern:

> Which issues can be addressed immediately, and which require a controlled investigation?

## Department Heads

Department heads argue that their workforce challenges are not comparable. Some roles are difficult to replace, some departments are larger, some rely on contract employment and some operate under different business pressures.

Analytical requirement:

Participants must distinguish workforce volume from proportional risk and must avoid treating one company-wide average as a complete diagnosis.

## Employees and Informal Feedback

The HR team has heard comments concerning career progression, manager behaviour, workplace flexibility, attendance decisions and unequal treatment. These comments have not been consolidated into a complete grievance, manager or policy dataset.

Evidence rule:

An allegation must not be presented as a confirmed cause without supporting evidence.

## Legal and Compliance Perspective

Management wants the review to consider current Bangladesh labour-law, rules, gazettes, government circulars and relevant regulatory developments. This requirement is separate from the supplied workforce data.

Participants must independently verify current legal or regulatory claims through official sources and explain whether the available company evidence is sufficient to assess compliance.

## Role flexibility

Participants may respond from any approved role track. Each role has a different decision authority and analytical emphasis, but all roles must:

- distinguish evidence from opinion;
- identify uncertainty;
- avoid fabricated data;
- recommend controlled next steps;
- define how management should measure progress.

# Policy, Governance and Legal Research

This requirement is separate from the supplied workforce dataset.

Participants must not assume that the project data proves legal compliance or non-compliance. The project does not contain a complete policy library, attendance register, wage register, disciplinary record, leave register, grievance register, standing orders, manager approval history or statutory filing record.

## Required research standard

Any legal or regulatory statement must be verified against a current official source available on the submission date.

Preferred source types include:

- Bangladesh government legislation or gazette publications;
- official ministry or department publications;
- official rules, notifications or circulars;
- recognised statutory or regulatory authority publications.

Secondary commentary may be used for orientation, but it must not replace the official source.

## Required report section

Include a section titled:

```text
Policy, Governance and Legal Considerations
```

For each material issue, provide:

| Item | Required response |
|---|---|
| Issue | The policy, governance or legal question under review |
| Official source | Title, issuing authority, publication date and URL |
| Date accessed | Date on which the source was checked |
| Case relevance | Why the issue may matter to Hossain Group |
| Evidence available | Which existing project fields are relevant |
| Evidence missing | Which company records would be required |
| Assessment | Supported, plausible, not assessable or not applicable |
| Recommended control | Policy, approval, documentation, training or monitoring response |

## Topics that may require review

Participants should select only the topics relevant to their role and findings. Possible areas include:

- working time, attendance and overtime controls;
- leave administration;
- disciplinary and grievance procedures;
- contract and employment-status documentation;
- separation and termination records;
- wage, benefit and payment documentation;
- workplace safety and welfare obligations;
- equal and consistent policy administration;
- record retention and auditability;
- management approval authority;
- HR data governance and confidentiality.

This list is not a statement that a breach occurred.

## Evidence rule

A legal requirement and a workforce pattern are not the same thing.

Participants must distinguish:

1. what the law or regulation requires;
2. what the current project evidence shows;
3. what organisational record is missing;
4. whether compliance can actually be assessed.

Where the evidence is insufficient, state:

> **Insufficient evidence to conclude.**

## Prohibited approach

Do not:

- cite an unverified social-media post as legal authority;
- present an outdated provision as current without checking;
- claim a violation solely from turnover data;
- invent company policies or statutory records;
- provide a legal conclusion beyond the available evidence.

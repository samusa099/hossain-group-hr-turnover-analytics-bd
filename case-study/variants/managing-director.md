# Managing Director Track

## Role position

You are the final executive decision owner. You are not required to perform every calculation personally, but you are responsible for the quality of the final position and the consequences of acting on incomplete information.

## Management task

Prepare a board-ready response that determines:

- whether the workforce situation requires immediate intervention;
- which matters should be delegated for investigation;
- which management claims should be challenged;
- which policy or governance controls require executive attention;
- which decisions should be deferred until better evidence exists.

## Required outputs

- one-page executive position;
- decision register;
- enterprise risk priorities;
- 90-day action portfolio;
- 180-day review framework;
- management accountability matrix;
- policy and legal research summary;
- evidence limitations.

## Decision register format

| Decision | Evidence | Uncertainty | Owner | Timing | Review measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Executive standard

Your submission must make a decision without presenting uncertainty as certainty.

You must identify:

- what management should approve now;
- what management should investigate;
- what management should stop assuming;
- what evidence must be available at the next review.

# HR Business Partner Track

## Role position

You advise management on department-level workforce risk, employee relations and targeted intervention. You must work with managers without accepting their explanations automatically.

## Management task

Assess whether current workforce patterns justify targeted action and determine which concerns require further investigation.

## Required outputs

- department and available-segment assessment;
- evidence, hypothesis and data-gap matrix;
- management-claim review;
- targeted intervention priorities;
- employee-relations follow-up plan;
- manager-engagement plan;
- 30-day, 60-day and 90-day actions;
- measurement framework.

## Evidence matrix format

| Management concern | Current evidence | Status | Additional evidence | Proposed response |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Status must be one of:

- supported;
- plausible but unconfirmed;
- not assessable;
- not material to the selected analysis.

## HRBP standard

Do not treat an exit reason as a complete root cause. Explain how you would validate the issue through controlled follow-up, additional records or structured employee and manager review.

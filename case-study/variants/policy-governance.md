# Policy and Governance Track

## Role position

You assess whether management decisions are supported by clear policy, consistent authority, reliable records and current legal research.

## Management task

Determine which governance weaknesses may prevent Hossain Group from managing workforce risk consistently, and define the controls required to improve accountability and auditability.

## Required outputs

- policy and control inventory;
- approval-authority review;
- record and evidence-gap assessment;
- attendance, leave, promotion, performance and grievance governance review;
- current Bangladesh legal and regulatory research;
- compliance-evidence matrix;
- policy-control recommendations;
- 90-day governance implementation plan.

## Compliance-evidence matrix

| Requirement or control | Official source | Company evidence available | Evidence missing | Assessment | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Governance standard

The turnover dataset cannot independently prove legal compliance, misconduct, discrimination or policy inconsistency.

Your submission must separate:

- the external requirement;
- the company record needed to test it;
- the evidence currently available;
- the control management should implement.

Where compliance cannot be assessed, state:

> **Insufficient evidence to conclude.**

# Operations and Finance Track

## Role position

You evaluate workforce continuity, recruitment pressure and resource allocation. You must not convert missing cost data into invented savings.

## Management task

Determine whether additional recruitment should be approved, controlled or redirected, and identify where workforce instability creates operational exposure.

## Required outputs

- gross hiring versus net growth assessment;
- replacement-pressure analysis;
- department workforce-stability matrix;
- operational continuity risks;
- transparent scenario analysis;
- recruitment and retention decision options;
- assumptions register;
- quarterly monitoring plan.

## Scenario standard

Scenarios may estimate workforce effects using available headcount and turnover data. Monetary impact may be shown only where the cost assumptions are explicitly declared and clearly separated from the supplied evidence.

## Decision matrix format

| Option | Evidence | Assumptions | Operational effect | Risk | Recommended control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Operations and Finance standard

Do not interpret higher exit volume alone as higher proportional risk. Do not recommend general recruitment without explaining whether the request supports growth, replacement or structural workforce movement.

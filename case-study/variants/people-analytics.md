# People Analytics Track

## Role position

You are responsible for evidence quality, analytical method and decision-ready reporting. Your work must be reproducible and must not create certainty that the data does not support.

## Management task

Build an analytical position that validates the official metrics, identifies material workforce patterns and explains the limits of the evidence.

## Required outputs

- source and field review;
- data-quality assessment;
- metric reconciliation;
- workforce-movement analysis;
- relevant segmentation;
- dashboard or analytical report;
- evidence-quality register;
- documented limitations;
- recommended future HR data model.

## Required analytical distinctions

Your report must distinguish:

- gross hires from net workforce growth;
- exit volume from turnover exposure;
- structural movement from potentially preventable movement;
- association from causation;
- available evidence from unavailable management data.

## Reproducibility standard

Where an optional notebook is submitted:

- calculations must be reproducible;
- paths must reference the existing repository files;
- no dataset copy may be embedded;
- execution outputs must be cleared;
- assumptions must be visible and justified.

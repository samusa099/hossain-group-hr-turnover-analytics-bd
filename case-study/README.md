## Purpose

This case converts the existing Hossain Group turnover project into a controlled business simulation. Participants must work with incomplete evidence, competing management explanations, policy uncertainty and operational pressure.

The case does not provide a model answer. It requires each participant to establish what the available evidence supports, what remains uncertain and what management should do next.

## Final objective

> Prepare a management-ready position that separates evidence, uncertainty and required action, and supports a defensible decision for Hossain Group.

## Evidence boundary

The official evidence remains in the existing project folders, including:

- `data/raw/employee_master.csv`
- `data/processed/`
- `data/metadata/data_dictionary.json`
- `excel/`
- `powerbi/`
- `notebooks/`
- `looker_studio/`

No alternative employee dataset, salary dataset, attendance dataset, manager dataset or legal dataset is supplied with this case.

Where the evidence does not support a conclusion, use the statement:

> **Insufficient evidence to conclude.**

## Responsible-use requirement

Hossain Group is a fictional project organisation. All employee records are synthetic. Participants must not add real employee information, confidential company information, credentials or private organisational records.

# Metric Definitions

## Opening Headcount

Employees who joined on or before the period start and had not exited before the period start.

## Closing Headcount

Employees who joined on or before the period end and remained employed after the period end.

## Average Headcount

```text
(Opening Headcount + Closing Headcount) / 2
```

## Period Turnover Rate

```text
Total Exits during Period / Average Headcount
```

## Annualized Turnover Rate

```text
Period Turnover Rate × 12 / Number of Months in Period
```

## Monthly Turnover Rate

```text
Monthly Exits / Monthly Average Headcount
```

## Interpretation Caution

Turnover should be reviewed with department size, exit reason, hiring volume, workforce seasonality, contract completion, and business restructuring. A high rate is a signal for investigation, not an automatic conclusion of poor HR performance.
